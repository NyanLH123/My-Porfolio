My Journey

Scroll through the milestones that have shaped my path

2016 - Age 10
First Computer Experience
Got my first computer and became fascinated with how technology works. Started exploring basic programming and digital tools, sparking a lifelong passion for technology and innovation.
2019
High School Studies
Pursued science stream with focus on mathematics and physics. Started learning programming languages independently and participated in academic competitions. Developed leadership skills through student council.
2021
University & Community Service
Enrolled in Bachelor of Computing program. Simultaneously started volunteering with For The Future – Myanmar, discovering my passion for social impact and community development alongside technical learning.
2022
Professional Development
Became Programme Development Officer at ASEAN Youth Organization. Completed multiple online certifications in web development and machine learning. Started freelancing and contributing to open-source projects.
2023
Specialization & Impact
Deepened expertise in AI/ML through advanced courses and projects. Coordinated major community projects reaching 500+ beneficiaries. Promoted to senior roles in volunteer organizations while maintaining academic excellence.
2024 - Present
Building Impact at Scale
Working as Junior Web Developer at tech company while continuing volunteer leadership. Building innovative projects that bridge technology and social impact. Preparing for transition into AI/ML roles and entrepreneurship.
