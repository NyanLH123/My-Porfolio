Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What types of opportunities are you looking for?

I'm interested in roles that combine technology with social impact. This includes full-stack development positions, AI/ML opportunities, and projects that create positive community change. I'm also open to freelance collaborations and mentoring opportunities.

What is your typical response time?

I aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during weekdays. If you have urgent matters, please mention it in your subject line. You can also reach out via LinkedIn for faster communication if needed.

Are you available for freelance projects?

Yes! I take on selective freelance projects that align with my interests and schedule. I specialize in web development, API integration, and small-scale ML implementations. Please provide project details for a quote.

Can you mentor or give advice?

Absolutely! I'm passionate about helping others grow in tech and personal development. I offer informal mentoring sessions on programming, career guidance, and community work. Let's discuss what you need help with.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes, I'm experienced in working with international teams and clients. I'm available for remote collaborations and am flexible with time zone differences. My primary timezone is GMT+6:30 (Myanmar Standard Time).

How can I stay updated on your work?

Follow me on LinkedIn and GitHub for regular updates on projects, insights, and opportunities. I occasionally share about my journey, learnings, and open positions. Feel free to connect and drop a message anytime!
